Our Story

Built on trust, grown through every celebration we've touched

Events by Luwa is a corporate entity established in December 2017 and registered under the Corporate Affairs Commission. Over years in operation, our clientele has spanned a wide spectrum of business organisations and individuals.

We are specialised professionals in event planning, consulting, venue scouting, coordination and management. We also offer professional host and hostess services for every kind of occasion.

We commit to working with your budget to bring your dream event to reality. Your needs and vision are foremost in every decision we make, and we pay attention to every detail to ensure your event runs seamlessly and colourfully.

To relieve our clients of stress, and create beautiful, fulfilling memories.
