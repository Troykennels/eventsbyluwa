Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. This explains what we do with the information you share when you get in touch.

Who we are

Events by Luwa is a premium event planning and management company serving the United Kingdom and clients worldwide. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +44 7823 683189 or by email at eventsbyluwa22@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our form, WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and information about the event you are planning. We only ask for what helps us plan your celebration well.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, arrange your consultation, prepare a proposal and, if you go ahead, plan and coordinate your event. We do not sell your information to anyone.

Who we share it with

To deliver your event we may share relevant details with trusted vendors such as venues, caterers or stylists, and only what they need to play their part. We do this with your knowledge as your plans take shape.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to serve you and to meet any legal or accounting requirements, then we remove it.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we later add visitor analytics to understand how the site is used, we will update this page to say so.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Just message us and we will take care of it.
