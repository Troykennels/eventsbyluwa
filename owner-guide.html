Adding your beautiful work is simple

You do not need any code. You upload photos into a folder, and your website gallery updates itself within about a minute.

Add photos to the gallery

  1. Open your website project at github.com/Troykennels/eventsbyluwa and sign in.
  2. Click the assets folder, then gallery, then the folder for the kind of event, for example weddings.
  3. Click Add file, then Upload files. Drag your photos in, or choose them from your computer.
  4. Scroll down and click the green Commit changes button.
  5. Wait about a minute, then open your Gallery page and refresh. Your new photos are live.

Your gallery categories are:

WeddingsBirthdaysCorporateAnniversariesSurprise PartiesBurialsUsheringVendor Management

A few tips for the best look

  1. Name the file after the event. The file name becomes the caption, so lagos-garden-wedding.jpg reads nicely.
  2. Keep photos around 1600 pixels wide and under about 500 KB so pages stay fast.
  3. Landscape and portrait both work. The gallery arranges them for you.
  4. To remove a photo, open the same folder, click the photo, then the bin icon, and commit.
You must view the changes on your live web address, not by opening a file on your computer. The live gallery reads the uploaded photos automatically.
